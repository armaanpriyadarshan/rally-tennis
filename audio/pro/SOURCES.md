# Rally Pro recorded sounds — sources

`sfx.ogg` (index `sfx.json`) is cut from these recordings on Freesound (freesound.org).
Every one is CC0 (public domain dedication): no attribution is required, and they are listed here anyway. They were trimmed,
high-passed, faded and brought to even levels; the clay bounces are the hard and grass bounces made duller with a little grit.
Built by scripts kept outside the repo (/tmp/rally-work/sfx/tools). No audio from any broadcast or video is used. The crowd is
the game's own earlier recording (../crowd.ogg), unchanged.
