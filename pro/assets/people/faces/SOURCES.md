# Sources of the players' faces

Each texture here is a Microsoft Rocketbox avatar's head texture (MIT) with one photograph of the player painted into the face by `tools/face_bake.py`:
the face found and matched to the head by its landmarks, lighting flattened, colour moved onto the head's own skin, blended in at the edges.
Hair, ears, neck and eyes are the avatar's. A texture made from a CC BY-SA photograph is itself under that licence; one made from a CC BY photograph carries its attribution.

# Heads

The heads under `assets/people/heads/` are Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (MIT; see `assets/people/LICENSE-Rocketbox.md`), cut to the head mesh with its skeleton and head textures by `tools/rb_head.py`.
